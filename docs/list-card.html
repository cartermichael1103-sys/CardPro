This tool never posts anything to eBay for you — it only generates a draft (title, description, suggested price) that you review and paste into eBay's own listing form yourself. Card ID is AI-generated and can be wrong; always double-check the details against your physical card before listing. Pricing is based on current active-listing asking prices, not sold prices.

This page isn't connected to a listing-draft backend yet. Set WORKER_URL at the top of list-card.js to your deployed Cloudflare Worker URL (see worker/README.md).
